Trattoria Italiana

Floor 2 | Food court | Monday to Sunday 12:00 to 22:00 hrs.

With an elegant yet welcoming atmosphere, the restaurant blends fresh ingredients, classic recipes, and a contemporary touch to create flavorful dishes. From handmade pasta and wood-fired pizzas to carefully selected wines, it is the perfect place to enjoy memorable moments within the exclusive Teocalli experience.

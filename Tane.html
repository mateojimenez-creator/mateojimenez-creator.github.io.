Tane

Floor 2 | Leisure | Monday to Sunday 10:00 to 22:00 hrs.

TANE stands as the pinnacle of Mexican luxury, a heritage design house where silver is transformed into art through decades of unparalleled craftsmanship. Its presence in the plaza brings an aura of sophistication and exclusivity, offering an impeccable curation of jewelry, home decor, and iconic pieces that celebrate Mexico’s cultural legacy with a contemporary aesthetic. More than just a boutique, it is a destination for those who seek excellence in detail and the intrinsic value of handmade mastery, solidifying the plaza’s status as a benchmark for distinction and refined taste.

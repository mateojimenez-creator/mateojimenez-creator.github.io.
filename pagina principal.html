Teocalli

Teocalli is a lifestyle destination that blends luxury, culture, and sustainability into a single immersive experience. Inspired by Mexico’s prehispanic heritage, the mall combines shopping, educational spaces, sports facilities, green areas, and cultural attractions in an architecturally striking environment. More than just a mall, Teocalli is designed to be a community where visitors can shop, learn, relax, and connect with history in a modern and innovative setting.

Our Services

TEPOCHCALLI

Educational Center

Teocalli’s Educational Center is a unique learning space designed to bring Mexico’s rich prehispanic heritage to life through immersive and interactive experiences. Unlike traditional classrooms, the center combines culture, history, science, and hands on activities to create an engaging environment where visitors of all ages can explore and learn in meaningful ways. Programs include workshops on prehispanic mathematics, astronomy, art, crafts, architecture, and indigenous traditions, all guided by trained educators and cultural specialists.

YAOTL

Gym

Teocalli’s Fitness Center offers a wellness experience designed to complement the mall's lifestyle focused vision. This modern gym combines good equipment, functional training spaces, and expert-led fitness programs within an environment inspired by the strength and discipline of ancient civilizations. Members can enjoy a variety of workout areas including cardio, strength training, group classes, and recovery zones. More than just a gym, the fitness center promotes health, balance, and community that creates a space where visitors can improve their physical well-being while enjoying the unique atmosphere that defines Teocalli.

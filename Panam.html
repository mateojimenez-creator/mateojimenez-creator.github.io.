Panam

Floor 1 | Department Store | Monday to Sunday 11:00 to 22:00 hrs.

**PANAM** is a vibrant icon of Mexican street culture, blending nostalgic heritage with the bold energy of contemporary urban fashion. Known for its unmistakable silhouette and kaleidoscopic colors, the brand has evolved from a classic schoolyard staple into a symbol of authentic self-expression and local pride. Its presence in the plaza offers a fresh, accessible take on streetwear, inviting everyone from sneaker enthusiasts to trendsetters to step into a pair of "Padrísimos" and walk with the unique rhythm and soul of modern Mexico.

Hermes

Floor 2 | Department Store | Monday to Sunday 10:00 to 22:00 hrs.

Experience the pinnacle of French craftsmanship and timeless elegance at **Hermès**, the plaza’s most prestigious destination for luxury. Founded on a legacy of equestrian excellence, the house offers an unparalleled selection of handcrafted leather goods—including the iconic Birkin and Kelly bags—alongside vibrant silk scarves, sophisticated ready-to-wear, and world-class perfumery. Every piece at Hermès is a testament to meticulous artistry and enduring style, inviting you into a world where heritage and innovation meet. Whether you are seeking a signature accessory or a masterpiece of design, Hermès provides a refined shopping experience defined by exclusivity, grace, and the beauty of the handmade.

Ghandi Bookstore

Floor 2 | Leisure | Monday to Sunday 10:00 to 19:00 hrs.

Immerse yourself in Mexico’s most iconic literary destination at **Librerías Gandhi**, where culture and curiosity meet. More than just a bookstore, our plaza location is a vibrant sanctuary for readers of all ages, offering an unmatched selection of Spanish and international titles across every genre imaginable—from deep philosophical works and contemporary fiction to children’s tales and academic texts. Beyond the shelves, discover a curated collection of music, film, and unique cultural gifts that have made Gandhi a reference point for intellectuals and dreamers for over 50 years. Whether you’re here to find your next great read, explore the latest releases, or simply get lost in the stacks, Gandhi provides a space where the world of ideas is always within reach.

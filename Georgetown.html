Dive into the soulful world of vinyl at Georgetown Records, the plaza’s destination for music purists and crate diggers alike. Specializing in high-quality used vinyl across every genre—from vintage jazz and classic rock to rare punk, garage, and world music—we offer a curated selection that celebrates the rich history of recorded sound. Whether you're searching for a nostalgic gem, a dazzling rarity to complete your collection, or a new favorite artist, our store provides an authentic, high-fidelity experience that digital playlists simply can’t match. Visit us to flip through our latest arrivals and rediscover the magic of analog in a space built for those who truly love music.

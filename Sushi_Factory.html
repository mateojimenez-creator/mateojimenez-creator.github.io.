Sushi Factory

Floor 2 | Leisure | Monday to Sunday 12:00 to 22:00 hrs.

Experience a fresh twist on Japanese tradition at **Sushi Factory**, the plaza’s go-to spot for innovative and delicious sushi. Known for our vibrant atmosphere and creative rolls, we blend high-quality ingredients with bold, contemporary flavors to satisfy every craving. From classic nigiri and sashimi to our signature "factory-style" rolls and savory hot dishes, each plate is prepared with precision and a touch of culinary flair. Whether you’re stopping in for a quick, healthy lunch or enjoying a relaxed dinner with friends, Sushi Factory offers a fun and flavorful dining experience that brings the best of modern sushi culture to your table.

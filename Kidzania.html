Kidzania

Floor 1 | Leisure | Monday to Sunday 10:00 to 22:00 hrs.

KidZania is an award-winning global phenomenon that brings a miniature, high-tech city to life, designed specifically to empower children through the magic of role-play. Within this hyper-realistic indoor theme park, kids explore a sprawling urban landscape where they can "work" in over 100 professions—from pilots and surgeons to firefighters and chefs—earning their own currency and learning the value of financial literacy. By blending education with pure entertainment, KidZania provides a safe, immersive environment where the leaders of tomorrow discover their passions and develop real-world skills, making it the ultimate family destination for inspiration and discovery.

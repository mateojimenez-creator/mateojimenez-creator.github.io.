Tardan

Floor 2 | Department Store | Monday to Sunday 11:00 to 22:00 hrs.

**Tardan** is a legendary symbol of elegance and tradition, boasting over 175 years of mastery in the art of fine hat-making. Located in the heart of the plaza, this iconic boutique bridges the gap between historical heritage and contemporary fashion, offering an exquisite collection ranging from classic felt fedoras to artisanal Panama hats. Each piece reflects a commitment to timeless style and superior quality, making Tardan an essential destination for those who seek to crown their personal look with the distinction and character that only a true master milliner can provide.
